Seasonal screen decorative icon assets
===================================
The SVG icon files in this directory are adapted from Lucide Icons:
https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide
Copyright (c) Lucide Icons and Contributors.
Licensed under the ISC License.
https://lucide.dev/license

The asset copies retain their original geometry; colors and stroke weights
were adjusted for CFI's seasonal scene artwork. The icons are served locally
with the time-clock site, without a third-party runtime dependency.
